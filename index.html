Professional Summary

The Art of
Refined Performance

Versatile and accomplished pianist and vocalist with over 10 years of professional experience in live performance, television productions, and hospitality entertainment. Classically trained with an ATCL (Distinction) from Trinity College London and AIWMS.

Performing piano and vocals simultaneously across a wide range of genres — jazz, pop, rock, boogie-woogie, and bossa nova. Proven experience in high-profile TV productions, luxury venue residencies, and private events, consistently delivering engaging and high-quality musical experiences for diverse audiences.
